<h1>Contributing</h1>

<p>Want to contribute? Open an issue first describing what you intend to do. It avoids duplicated effort and lets us align on direction before you spend time coding.</p>

<br>

<h2>Code Standards</h2>

<ul>
  <li><b>C++17</b> minimum</li>
  <li>Code in English. Comments can be in English or Portuguese</li>
  <li>Headers use <code>#pragma once</code></li>
  <li>Namespaces: <code>nocturne::core</code>, <code>nocturne::unity</code>, etc.</li>
  <li>No <code>using namespace</code> in headers</li>
  <li>Clear names, no obscure abbreviations</li>
  <li>Keep functions small and focused</li>
  <li>Null-check pointers before dereferencing</li>
</ul>

<br>

<h2>Pull Requests</h2>

<ol>
  <li>Fork the repository</li>
  <li>Create a branch: <code>git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name</code></li>
  <li>Commit with a descriptive message</li>
  <li>Push and open a Pull Request</li>
</ol>

<p>Your PR should include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>What changed and why</li>
  <li>How you tested it</li>
  <li>Any breaking changes</li>
</ul>

<br>

<h2>Reporting Bugs</h2>

<p>Include the following so we can reproduce and fix quickly:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Windows version</li>
  <li>Target game and Unity version</li>
  <li>Offsets and patterns you are using</li>
  <li>Steps to reproduce</li>
  <li>Logs or screenshots if applicable</li>
</ul>

<br>

<h2>Security Issues</h2>

<p>Do not open public issues for security vulnerabilities. Email the maintainers directly.</p>
